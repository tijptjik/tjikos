# Demo artwork

These original synthetic fixtures were created with the built-in image generation
tool on 2026-10-26. They contain no private media or external runtime dependencies.

## Two-sun shoreline

`demo-artwork.png` is fictional concept art for the Kepler photo and image demos:
a volcanic coastline, turquoise ocean, distant mountains and two distinct suns at
first light. It is imagined scenery, not an astronomical reconstruction of
Kepler-186f. The image demo also supplies a Wi-Fi icon to exercise sender-image
precedence in both Notjik and Dunst.

## Robot dog

`demo-robot-dog.gif` is a looping eight-frame, 384 × 384 animation at five frames
per second. Its generated 4 × 2 sprite sheet depicts the same ivory ceramic robot
dog with cyan eyes tilting its head, lifting a paw and wagging its segmented tail.
Frames were cropped and encoded with FFmpeg using a shared GIF palette. It is
used by the Telegram GIF demo; the smaller generic animation fixtures remain for
emoji and media-format acceptance tests.
